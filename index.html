Good to know

Questions, answered.

Anything not here? Email info@bighillretreat.com or call 902-295-2726.

Are you open in winter?
Yes. Big Hill Retreat is open year-round. Ask us about your dates.
How far is it from Baddeck?
About 12 km. From Route 105 (Trans-Canada), look for the Rear Big Hill sign, east of Exit 10.
How many people does each cabin sleep?
Ask us about beds and sleeping space for the cabin you like. Email or call and we'll tell you.
Can we swim or fish?
There are two small ponds on the property for swimming and fishing.
What's in the garden?
It's an organic garden, and there are extra vegetables for guests. Ask us what's growing when you come.
Can we see the pottery studio?
Big Hill Pottery is the family pottery studio. Ask us about it.
Do you host groups?
Ask us about group retreats.
Are pets welcome? Is there a minimum stay?
Please ask us: email or call before you book.
How do I book?
By email at info@bighillretreat.com or by phone at 902-295-2726 (toll free 1-888-356-0303). The planner below writes the email for you.
